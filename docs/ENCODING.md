# Encoding conventions

Use nominal records and explicit functions/queries for legal concepts. Sources,
instruments, provisions, revisions, cases, opinions, judgments, propositions,
treatments, findings, rights, duties, powers and proceedings are library data.
There is no universal legal rank, implicit retroactivity or automatic preemption.

An encoding record links a rule to source anchors, interpretation rationale,
assumptions and a review status. A citation does not activate an authority.
Use scope and applicability expressions to describe the encoded effect and
conditional priority declarations to resolve authored exceptions.

Treat provision identity, source-document identity, locator, revision identity
and encoding release as separate concepts. Keep old revisions. A saved earlier
event can remain governed by an older rule after an amendment. Separate adoption,
enactment, commencement, applicability, expiration and historical-authority
cutoffs; the engine has no implicit current date.

Findings and evidence remain supplied premises. Keep competing claims even
when selecting an accepted interpretation. Record the issue, decision-maker,
role, scope, evidence, rationale and supersession explicitly. A role label
does not authenticate competence or adjudication. Burdens, admissibility and
review standards require their own legal rules; do not replace them with
invented probabilities.

Precedent modeling separates litigation, reasoning, disposition, interpreted
propositions and later treatment. A treatment targets the relevant proposition,
opinion or judgment, with context and temporal reach. No whole-case valid flag
stands in for this analysis.

The initial authority package supports majority holdings in the two named
federal profiles and distinguishes concurrence/dissent. Per curiam, fractured,
en banc/panel precedence, Erie questions and unsupported forums require further
encoding. Citation permission and persuasive relevance are supplied analyses,
not inferred from publication alone. Unknown findings remain observations.

The Constitution package evaluates specified textual requirements, not overall
eligibility. Its voting queries answer numeric threshold questions only; they
do not decide quorum, valid votes, procedure or every Article V condition.
Amendment XX sections 1-2 commence on 1933-10-15; the separate section-4 example
uses ratification on 1933-01-23. Sources: [National Archives](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/amendments-11-27).
The Thornton query requires accepted classification and authority findings and
only addresses an additional state qualification for congressional service.

The IRA model uses the identified 2024 codification of sections 219, 408 and 408A,
reconciled to the dated 2025 Form 8606, its instructions and Notice 2024-80.
It does not claim an exhaustive 2025 legislative or precedent review.
Do not relabel the statutory source edition as 2025.

Contribution queries support a supplied aggregate of other personal IRA
contributions for the annual cap. Conversion allocation deliberately requires
that other aggregate to be zero, so no unmodeled current-year basis is omitted.
The conversion profile includes complete, uniquely identified relevant own
traditional/SEP/SIMPLE accounts, complete basis/transaction histories, qualifying
compensation and a nondeductible designation. It excludes inherited accounts,
RMDs, corrections, outstanding rollovers, other distributions, cross-year
transactions, special transactions, deduction phaseouts and state tax.

Exact taxable allocation and reporting projection are separate queries. The
projection uses six fractional decimal places at line 10, then the caller's
positive output quantum with HalfAwayFromZero. It is an explicit policy within
the form's at-least-three-place instruction, not a universal rounding rule.
See [2025 Form 8606](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/f8606--2025.pdf) and
[instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i8606--2025.pdf).

Legal consequences need authored rules. A prohibition does not automatically
create liability, invalidity, enforcement authority, damages or a remedy.
Compatible effects should be accumulated explicitly. A pure state-transition
function records the modeled consequences of supplied events; it performs no
governmental or private act.

Before expanding coverage, add positive, negative, boundary, missing-fact,
disputed-interpretation and temporal fixtures. Record both reviewed exclusions
and questions never investigated. Maintain independent expected answers in
fixtures; evaluator/checker agreement alone cannot establish domain correctness.
