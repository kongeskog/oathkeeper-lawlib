# Historical Seattle pilot

These notes describe the original pilot; they are not a current review or generated inventory.

## Question

- Under the supplied historical SMC 11.72.440 formalization, which arguments support or oppose a violation for one parking episode?

## Acceptance criteria

- Community contributors review source editions, scope, exceptions and competing interpretations; professional credentials are not required.
- Separate, attributed reviews of legal fidelity and formalization are bound to exact artifact digests.
- Collect additional authentic matters without inventing outcomes or publishing private records.
- Measure reviewer effort and missed conditions against a source-based checklist.
- Hold out distinct litigation matters and source families; the current single-matter fixtures do not measure generalization.

## Unmeasured outcomes

- review_minutes
- critical_omissions
- practitioner_agreement
- checklist_comparison

## Outside the modeled slice

- Full Chapter 11.30 impoundment procedure and prerequisites
- All other parking and traffic provisions
- Full homestead statutory scheme and exceptions
- All other Seattle Municipal Code titles and amendments
- City Charter
- Uncodified ordinances and emergency measures
- Agency rules and director rules
- Other applicable Washington, federal, tribal, and county authorities
- Full relevant precedent and subsequent treatment through the present

## Limitations

- Historical decision reconstruction, not a present-day validity certification
- Original-court findings are trusted evidence inputs, not independently recomputed opinions
- No independent legal reviewer has approved this encoding
- Scope predicates require proof; exemptions are not inferred from missing facts
- Five reconstructions are issues from one litigation matter, not five independent cases.

## Unresolved exceptions and definitions

- What counts as authorization
- Chapter definitions of both junk-vehicle categories
- Other-law applicability and full authority inventory
- Posted-sign enforcement under subsection A
- Impoundment procedure under Chapter 11.30
