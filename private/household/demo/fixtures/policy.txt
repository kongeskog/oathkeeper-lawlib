Fictional household policy, adopted October 18, 2026.
Section 1. This private policy commences January 1, 2029 and applies only to members and activities identified in the scenario. It makes no claim about public law.
Section 2. After-hours noise is prohibited. Section 2(b). Noise reasonably necessary for an emergency is expressly permitted; an accepted emergency finding is required to apply this exception.
Section 3. Beginning January 1, 2030, an approved event also permits after-hours noise. Section 2 remains applicable to conduct before that date, including later assessments of that conduct.
Section 4. A member who violates section 2 must receive a written notice within seven calendar days after the event. Exclude the event day; include the seventh day. The policy defines no fine, eviction power, or other remedy.
